# 🛡️ JobShield — Smart Job Scam Detection Platform

**Making online job searching safer, one opportunity at a time.**

JobShield is a web-based platform designed to help job seekers identify suspicious job offers, fraudulent recruiters, and recruitment scams. It aims to make job verification easier by highlighting potential warning signs and providing useful safety guidance.

## ✨ Key Features

- 🔍 **Scam Detection:** Analyze job offers to identify suspicious indicators.
- 🔗 **URL & Email Analysis:** Check links and recruiter contact details for potential red flags.
- 📄 **Job Offer Analysis:** Review job descriptions and offer-letter information.
- 🚨 **Scam Reporting:** Submit reports about suspected fraudulent opportunities.
- 📋 **Public Fraud Registry:** Help users stay informed about reported scams.
- 💰 **Salary Reality Check:** Compare advertised salaries with realistic expectations.
- 🔐 **Safety Awareness:** Learn how to avoid advance-fee fraud and identity theft.

## 🎯 Project Objective

To help students, freshers, and job seekers make informed decisions, recognize recruitment fraud, and approach online job opportunities more confidently.
