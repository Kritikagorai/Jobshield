🚨 The "Golden Hour" 4-Step Recovery Protocol

Action taken within the first 2 hours yields the highest probability of fund recovery:

1

Dial 1930 Immediately

Call National Cyber Helpline 1930. Provide your transaction UTR, debit bank name, and suspect UPI ID to initiate a quick account freeze.

2

Notify Bank Fraud Desk

Email your bank's Nodal Officer with the formal freeze notice below to place an immediate lien on the beneficiary account under RBI Master Directions.

3

File on cybercrime.gov.in

Copy the automated legal draft below and paste it into the official complaint portal at cybercrime.gov.in under Financial Job Fraud.

4

Preserve Chat Evidence

Export complete WhatsApp / Telegram chats as .txt, take screenshots of payment screens, and do NOT delete messages or block suspects yet.
